# Lean dependencies and source credits

[Lean 4.34.1](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/tree/v4.34.1) and [mathlib at d13f23b](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/tree/d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612) supply the language, library, and much of the categorical, algebraic and topological infrastructure. Lake fetches mathlib and the transitive dependencies recorded in [the manifest](lake-manifest.json); their source trees are not vendored here. Mathlib distributes its code under the [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612/LICENSE).
